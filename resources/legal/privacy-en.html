This policy applies to the SOSlive service: the SOSlive website (soslive.endorbit.hu) and the SOSlive Android and iOS mobile apps (together: SOSlive). We process personal data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and Hungarian Act CXII of 2011 on informational self-determination.

2. Summary

  • Your events (live stream link, location, messages, images) and your settings (e-mail addresses and phone numbers to notify) are stored in your own Google Drive, in a folder named SOSlive. The SOSlive server does not see or store them: the mobile app and your browser communicate with Google directly.
  • The SOSlive server stores only the minimum needed for sign-in: your name, e-mail address, Google account ID and the time of your last sign-in.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and we do not sell your data.

The SOSlive server does not store Google access tokens and has no access to your Google Drive.

3.2. Data stored in your own Google Drive

The following data is stored in your Google Drive, in the SOSlive folder. You control it: the SOSlive apps (mobile app, browser) read or write it only at your request and with your Google account's permission; it is never sent to the SOSlive server. You can delete it from your Drive at any time.

  • Events: start time, stream link (and, if you provide them, the link of your stream provider's page and of the recording), your location (every 30 seconds), messages, images.
  • Settings (config.json): e-mail addresses and phone numbers to notify, number of events to keep.
  • Sharing: the Google accounts you share your events with in the mobile app (kept by Google Drive).

3.3. Mobile app

When you start an event – only at your initiative and with your permission – the mobile app uses your device's location, camera and microphone. It sends the video to the stream provider you configured. It sends notifications (SMS, e-mail) from your device, on your behalf, to the recipients you specified. The mobile app does not communicate with the SOSlive server.

3.4. Other people's data

If you add people to notify, share your events with someone, or other people appear in your recording, you provide and control their data. Please do so only with their knowledge and consent. The SOSlive server never receives this data.

5. Public event links and sharing

  • Your event files are created with “anyone with the link can view” permission, so the people you notify can open the event page without signing in (soslive.endorbit.hu/e/…). Anyone who has the link can see the event (location, messages, images, stream link). Event pages are not indexed by search engines.
  • If you share your events with someone in the mobile app, they can see all your events (including future ones) with their Google account, but not your settings. You can revoke sharing in the mobile app.
  • Viewers' browsers download the event directly from Google; the SOSlive server stores no data about viewers (other than the server logs in section 3.1).

8. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). The server stores only the minimum necessary data and no Google tokens or event data. The administration interface is password protected and accessible only to the controller.
